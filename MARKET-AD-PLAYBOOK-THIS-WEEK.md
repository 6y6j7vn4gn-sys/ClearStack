## 3. Advertise + outreach playbook (organic only)

### A. Warm network (highest conversion — do daily)
**Script:** *“Approximate page count? Fixed ClearStack package — searchable PDF + index. $595 / $1,250 / $1,950. 50% to start.”*  
Sources: ex-colleagues (construction/PM), ABC/contractor breakfasts, church/community admins, bookkeepers.  
**Quota:** 3 warm asks / ClearStack block.

### B. Google Business Profile (organic)
- Category: **Document scanning service** · service-area (no home address)  
- Paste pack: `email/GBP-PASTE-PACK.md` + keywords from `SEO-HOT-WORDS.md`  
- Photos: process, redacted before/after, OCR search screenshot, labeled box  
- **Weekly post cadence (copy below)** — 1 post / week minimum; 2 if capacity

#### GBP posts (4 ready — rotate)
1. **Closeout:** “IE project teams: turn closeout binders into a searchable PDF book — OCR + index, fixed price by page count. ClearStack · Upland/Ontario/RC.”  
2. **HOA:** “Board packets buried in binders? ClearStack builds searchable digital books for HOA/property admin files. Quote from page count.”  
3. **Escrow:** “Closed-file rooms → searchable packets. Mail-in or appointment drop-off. ClearStack IE.”  
4. **Offer:** “Packages: ≤50p $595 · 50–200 $1,250 · 200–500 $1,950. Messy stack in. Clear packet out.”

### C. LinkedIn organic (ClearStack brand only)
- Headline idea: *ClearStack · Searchable PDF packages · Inland Empire*  
- **No personal legal name** on posts, creatives, or company page  
- 2 posts / week + 5 title connects (PM, Office Manager, Community Manager, Escrow Manager, Paralegal Supervisor)

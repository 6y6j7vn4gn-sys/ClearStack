## Deck spine (Gamma-ready bullets)

### Slide 1 — Title
- ClearStack
- Messy stack in. Clear packet out.
- Searchable digital books for the Inland Empire
- Upland · Ontario · Rancho Cucamonga · nearby IE

### Slide 2 — What we sell (not a copy shop)
- Paper → searchable PDF packages (OCR = optical character recognition)
- Named files + index = a digital book, not a photo dump
- Mail-in · appointment drop-off · secure upload
- Secure link delivery · optional USB flash drive

### Slide 3 — Who buys (top 3)
- Property management / HOA — lease, vendor, board archives
- GCs & closeout teams — binders, submittals, bid files
- Escrow / title / RE admin — closed-file rooms before move or audit
- Decision titles: PM, Office Manager, Escrow Ops, Doc Control, Board Treasurer

### Slide 4 — Pricing lock
- ≤50 pages → $595
- 50–200 → $1,250
- 200–500 → $1,950
- Rush +40% when capacity allows
- 50% deposit to start

### Slide 5 — How to advertise (organic only until first invoice)
- Warm network first (highest conversion)
- GBP (Google Business Profile) weekly posts + keywords
- LinkedIn organic — page-count ask, not pitch-deck dumps
- Partnerships after first invoice
- Cold-light 5–10/day max
- Zero paid ads until first invoice

### Slide 6 — Soft-live ~70%
- Sell on apps before domain if needed
- Prioritize channels that work now: warm · LinkedIn · phone · GBP draft
- Zelle + SEND unlock conversion (deposit + outreach leaving drafts)
- Domain / Stripe / filings = harden, not Day-One blockers

### Slide 7 — 7-day ad rotation (one beat each)
- Day 1 HOA/PM — lease boxes → searchable books
- Day 2 GC — closeout binders that hand off clean
- Day 3 Escrow/title — closed-file room → searchable packet
- Day 4 Church/nonprofit — board packs you can find
- Day 5 General IE — paper to searchable PDF
- Day 6 PM/HOA — find the vendor file before the meeting
- Day 7 GC — owner handoff without the paper chase
- CTA every day: approximate page count → quote

### Slide 8 — Outreach lock
- Warm text · cold LinkedIn note · 5-sentence email · 30-sec phone · Day-3 follow-up
- Always: ClearStack brand · page count · 50% deposit
- Never: personal consulting brand on public creatives

### Slide 9 — Market tip (improve week over week)
- Ship 5 asks > rewrite the site again
- Log touches Fridays · kill channels that don’t reply
- One redacted before/after Ctrl+F demo (no fake client docs)
- After first deposit: GBP review ask · scanner from revenue

### Slide 10 — Close / CTA
- Clear the stack. Keep the signal.
- Ask for a quote — send approximate page count
- ClearStack · Inland Empire

**What “big” means here:** Urgent **50–500 page** packs — not thousand-box warehousing. ClearStack wins on speed, fixed scope, and a searchable book (named files + index), not commodity scan dumps.

## 5. Market tip — improve week over week

1. **Ship outreach before polish.** 5 page-count asks beat one more rewrite of the site.
2. **Log every touch:** date · segment · title · interest Y/N · page estimate · next step. Review Fridays.
3. **One redacted before/after PDF** (Ctrl+F demo) — best sales weapon; no fake client docs.
4. **Ask better intake:** book vs packets · document types · special instructions · return vs dispose.
5. **GBP weekly post + 1 LinkedIn post** from the 7-day ad pack; reuse winners.
6. **After first deposit:** claim review ask · unlock ScanSnap from revenue · then light partnerships.
7. **Kill what fails:** if cold email ≤2% reply after 40 sends, shift time to warm + GBP + partners.
8. **Soft-live → hard-live path:** Zelle handle → SEND → inbox → domain → Stripe → filings — one unlock per week, not all at once.

## Always ask: dispose vs send back
At **order** and again at **delivery**:

> After you approve the digital book, should we:  
> **A)** Return the originals to you (ship or pickup)  
> **B)** Securely dispose/shred via partner (certificate when available)  
> **C)** Hold 14 days then dispose if we don’t hear back  

Never shred before client written OK on the digital delivery.

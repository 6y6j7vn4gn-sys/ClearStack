## Acronym glossary (what we’ve been saying)

| Short | Full words | What it means for us |
|-------|------------|----------------------|
| **DBA** | Doing Business As | Legal trade name “ClearStack” so the bank/clients don’t need your personal name on the brand |
| **EIN** | Employer Identification Number | Free IRS business tax ID (like a Social Security number for the business) |
| **LLC** | Limited Liability Company | Optional legal company form; costs ~$70 to file + ~$800/year California tax |
| **FBN** | Fictitious Business Name | Same idea as DBA in San Bernardino County — the ~$90 filing |
| **PMB** | Private Mail Box | Your box number at the UPS Store (not a home address) |
| **OCR** | Optical Character Recognition | Software that makes a scanned PDF *searchable* (you can find words) |
| **GBP** | Google Business Profile | Free Google Maps / “near me” listing for ClearStack |
| **ADF** | Automatic Document Feeder | Scanner tray that feeds pages by itself (ScanSnap has this) |
| **COGS** | Cost of Goods Sold | Direct costs of a job (USB, shipping, labels) — subtracted before “profit” |
| **CS-ID** | ClearStack Job ID | Code like `CS-20260906-01` on every job / Zelle memo |
| **CTA** | Call To Action | The “Ask for a quote” button / next step we want the client to take |

## Market lesson (ClearStack)

**What people buy:** Not “cheap scans.” They buy **time + findability** — a binder that becomes a searchable digital book.

**Who pays best (our Week-1 targets):**
1. **GCs** (General Contractors) — closeout / bid binders  
2. **HOA / property managers** — board packs, vendor files  
3. **Escrow / title** — closed file rooms  

**How the market prices:**
- Commodity shops: cents per page (race to the bottom)  
- **Us:** fixed packages by page count ($595 / $1,250 / $1,950) — premium, clear, modern  

**Why we win locally:** Inland Empire + human operator + AI packet build (Man × Machine) + commercial mailbox + professional Zelle/Stripe.

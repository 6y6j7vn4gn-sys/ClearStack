Discount & payment

  • −15% off package total applies only to Zelle payments.
  • Visa / Mastercard: pay via Stripe / Payment Links at full list price (no discount).
  • Zelle: −15% off package total, then 50% deposit of the discounted total. Zelle to ClearStack — token after Chase Business (no invented token on this site).

Zelle example (after −15%, then 50% deposit): Starter package $75.65 → deposit ~$37.83 · Binder $143.65 → ~$71.83 · Multi $254.15 → ~$127.08 · Box $339.15/box → ~$169.58.

Card / Stripe 50% deposits at list: Starter $44.50 · Binder $84.50 · Multi $149.50 · Box $199.50.

Terms

  • 50% deposit on intake — Zelle: 50% of the −15% discounted package; cards: 50% of list price. Balance due on digital proof approval.
  • Work starts after deposit clears.
  • Prep Heavy and partner services are quoted separately when needed.

Trust & security

We practice chain of custody on every batch. NDAs are available. HIPAA-conscious handling and a BAA when required — we do not claim “HIPAA certified.” After approval, originals can be returned or destroyed via certified shredding with a Certificate of Destruction.

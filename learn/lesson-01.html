What ClearStack sells (and what it is not)

We do not sell “cheap scans.” We sell a searchable digital book — OCR so every page is findable — priced as a clear package.

The offer in plain words

  • OCR (Optical Character Recognition) = software that turns a photo of paper into text you can search.
  • Package = fixed price by page band, not mystery hourly piles for clean stacks.
  • Man×Machine = you scan when needed; Money Maker names, indexes, OCR QA, final packet.

What ClearStack is NOT

  • Not Iron Mountain long-term storage
  • Not a printer shop — we sell searchable packets
  • Not your legal / WCAB / SSA lane on the public site
  • Not under your personal name — public brand is ClearStack only
  • Not your home porch — intake is UPS Store PMB, never 371 D St public

Who does what

YOU (Operator)

  • Final send: FILE IT / SEND / BUY IT / GBP LIVE
  • Rent PMB · bank · sign-in walls
  • ≥3 job apps/day (Job Machine helps; you click)
  • Scan batches when paper arrives

ME (Money Maker)

  • OCR / name / index / QA
  • Drafts, site, quotes, scoreboard
  • Never invent deposits or SEND counts
  • Ask you only for final send

What ClearStack sells (and what it is not)

We do not sell “cheap scans.” We sell a searchable digital book — OCR so every page is findable — priced as a clear package.

The offer in plain words

  • OCR (Optical Character Recognition) = software that turns a photo of paper into text you can search.
  • Package = fixed price by page band, not mystery hourly piles for clean stacks.
  • Man×Machine = you scan; Money Maker names, indexes, OCR QA, final packet.

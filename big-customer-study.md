# ClearStack — Big-Customer Study Brief
**Audience:** Money Maker + Market Research  
**Market:** Inland Empire / Upland, CA (+ Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, Fontana, Riverside, San Bernardino; selective LA/OC)  
**Public brand:** ClearStack only  
**Offer lock:** Fixed-scope Doc→searchable OCR PDF packages. Adjacent: construction/project file packaging, blueprint/plan scanning. Not a copy shop. Not day-one HIPAA mega-bureau.

---

## 1. Executive summary (Money Maker)

- IE demand for digitization is real and local: population-serving offices (healthcare, legal/CPA, property mgmt, gov-adjacent) still sit on boxes; competitors already sell medical/legal/RE/HR, blueprints, shred+scan, schools, construction plans.
- ClearStack’s wedge is **fixed-scope box→OCR PDF packages** with clear handoff — beat walk-in (UPS/FedEx) on organization/searchability, beat national bureaus on local responsiveness and price transparency for mid-size jobs.
- Near-zero cash: win **repeatable mid packages** first (property mgmt / HOA leases & files; GC & trade project closeout sets; title/escrow & small law file rooms) before regulated medical backfiles or municipal RFPs.
- Competitors win on **facility footprint + compliance claims + onsite crews**; they lose on **slow quotes, overkill for 1–10 boxes, and impersonal walk-in quality**. That gap is ClearStack’s lane.
- Attack order under cash constraint: (1) property management / HOA, (2) construction GCs & subcontractors (plans + project files), (3) title/escrow + small law — then expand. **Zero paid ads until first invoice.**

### Package definitions (ClearStack speak)

| Package | What buyer gets | ClearStack posture |
|--------|-----------------|--------------------|
| **One-time box digitization** | Prep → scan → OCR searchable PDF package, fixed scope by box/page band | Core day-one SKU |
| **Ongoing scan-on-demand** | Retain/return physical + pull/scan as needed | Offer only when ops capacity exists; competitors (e.g. My ScanTeam) already sell this |
| **Blueprint / plan sets** | Large-format scan to PDF/TIFF; optional project-file bundling | Adjacent SKU; compete selectively vs ARC / walk-in |
| **Discovery / litigation dumps** | High-volume, labeled, searchable PDF sets for counsel | Mid-fit; emphasize organization & chain notes — not e-discovery platform |
| **Patient chart backfiles** | Clinic chart conversion | **Do not pitch as HIPAA-compliant until posture is real** |

> **Regulated verticals (medical, some school student records, certain gov PII):** need documented compliance posture (BAAs, access controls, retention/destruction policy, staff training) **before pitching**. Until then: exclude from outbound or frame only as “general office paper → PDF” with explicit scope exclusions for PHI/student records.

## 4. Competitor targeting patterns

| Competitor type | Examples (known) | How they win | How they lose / gap for ClearStack |
|-----------------|------------------|--------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Local scan bureau** | My ScanTeam (Ontario); Scan N More (Corona); Turn Source Imaging; Scandoc Imaging; DRM | Vertical menus (medical/legal/RE/HR), onsite crews, storage + on-demand, San Bernardino County footprint | Overbuilt for 1–5 boxes; quote friction; “facility” feel vs personal fixed-scope package |
| **AEC print + scan** | ARC Riverside / San Bernardino | Blueprint + print + project workflow (SKYSITE-class tools), GC relationships, walk-in large format | Price/complexity for small plan sets; not optimized for “messy office boxes → clean OCR packet” |
| **Walk-in retail** | UPS / FedEx (Upland & nearby) | Convenience, same-day small jobs, no sales cycle | Poor indexing/OCR packaging; no project management; quality inconsistent for multi-box archives |
| **Network / national** | Record Nations–style directories, large shred+scan brands | Compliance branding, storage contracts, multi-location | Slow, expensive, impersonal for IE SMB mid-jobs |

**Targeting pattern takeaway:** Competitors hunt **medical, legal, RE/financial, HR, blueprints, schools, construction plans, shred+scan**. ClearStack should **mirror the vertical list for awareness** but **sell a narrower SKU** (fixed-scope searchable PDF packages) where bureaus are slow and walk-ins are sloppy.

## 7. Do-not list

1. **Brand:** Never put personal names, Fakhouri Consulting, or home addresses on public materials, outreach templates, GBP, or proposals — **ClearStack only**.
2. **Compliance overclaim:** Do not claim HIPAA, FERPA, CJIS, or “government certified” unless true and documented. Flag regulated verticals; get posture before pitching PHI/student records.
3. **Positioning:** Not a copy shop; not day-one bulk HIPAA mega-bureau; not shredding-primary; not inventing named customers for case studies.
4. **Inventory / ops:** Do not take unlimited onsite storage or “scan-on-demand warehouse” commitments until capacity and chain-of-custody process exist.
5. **Paid ads:** **Zero paid ads until first invoice** (no Google/Meta boosts, no sponsored LinkedIn).
6. **Scope creep:** Do not promise full DMS implementations, EHR migrations, or ARC-class print programs in the core offer.

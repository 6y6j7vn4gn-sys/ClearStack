# ClearStack — Standard mail services · Drop-in · Drop-out
**Brand:** ClearStack · First-client friendly · Approachable

## A. DROP-IN (customer → us) — how paper arrives

### 1) Ship with standard carriers (most common first jobs)
| Carrier | Best for | How client uses it | Notes for ClearStack |
|---------|----------|--------------------|----------------------|
| **USPS** | Light–medium boxes, Priority / Ground Advantage | Buy label at usps.com or PO counter | Cheapest often; tracking; slower than UPS/FedEx sometimes |
| **UPS** | Heavier banker boxes | ups.com or UPS Store drop | Strong tracking; many IE drop points |
| **FedEx** | Medium–heavy, timed | fedex.com or FedEx Office | Good for deadlines; Office locations everywhere |
| **Amazon Shipping / Pirate Ship** | Discount labels | Client prints label at home | Client still pays; we don’t provide prepaid Day One |

**ClearStack Day One process**
1. Quote + 50% deposit  
2. We email: packing checklist + **printable 4×6 label** (CS- ID) + intake address (shared only after deposit — never publish home on website)  
3. Client packs → seals → affixes label → **drops at carrier** or schedules pickup with *their* carrier account  
4. We email receipt when box scans in / arrives  

**Who pays shipping in?** Client (standard). Optional later: prepaid return label cost added to invoice.

### 2) Drop-in (physical handoff — no carrier)
| Method | How it works | When to offer |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| **Scheduled IE drop-off** | Client books a 15-min window (Zoom/calendar). Hands box at agreed public-safe meet OR studio appointment. | Local Upland/Ontario/RC clients |
| **UPS Store / FedEx Office hold** | Client ships *to* a UPS Store / FedEx Office near us for hold-for-pickup — we collect. | If we don’t want home address on label |
| **Lobby / reception drop** (later) | Partner office receives for ClearStack. | After partner deal |
| **Pickup at client** | We drive to them. | Phase 2 after cash — quote trip fee |

**Not Day One:** open walk-in retail counter (looks like UPS/FedEx; wrong brand).

## B. DROP-OUT (us → customer) — how product leaves

### 1) Digital (always offer — included)
| Method | What | Cost |
|--------|------|------|
| **Secure link** | Drive / OneDrive / encrypted download | **Included** |
| **Email notice** | “Your searchable book is ready” + link | Included |

### 2) Physical mail-out (optional paid)
| Method | What ships | Carrier | ClearStack add-on |
|--------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Flash drive kit** | USB in branded sleeve + printed index in rigid/bubble mailer | USPS Priority or UPS | **+$35** (media + ship + handle) |
| **Return originals** | Repacked paper box | Same carriers | **At-cost freight + $25 handle** (or client prepaid label) |
| **Both** | Link + USB; paper separate | — | Link free + USB $75 + return freight |

### 3) Drop-out handoff (no carrier)
| Method | How | When |
|--------|-----|------|
| **Scheduled IE pickup by client** | Client collects USB and/or originals at appointment | Local |
| **We deliver USB locally** | Short drive radius | Quote small trip fee later |
| **Hold for pickup at partner store** | Rare | Only if arranged |

## C. Dispose path (not mail — still “drop-out” of paper)
After client **approves** digital book:
- Partner shred / certificate  
- Or return box via mail (above)  
- Or hold 14 days  

Never destroy before written OK.

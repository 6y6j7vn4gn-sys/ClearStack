1. What this business is / is not

Is

ClearStack turns messy paper — binders, boxes, stacks — into a searchable PDF packet: Optical Character Recognition (OCR) so clients can find any page, consistent file names, an index, and optional flash-drive mail-back.

Clients are local businesses and offices that need findable archives for bids, audits, handoffs, and board packs. Service area: Inland Empire — Upland, Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, and nearby.

Is not

  • Not a commodity “cents-per-page” copy shop race.
  • Not a walk-in retail counter.
  • Not legal advice, court filing, benefits advocacy, or case work.
  • Not a personal consulting brand on public pages.
  • Not a home-address business on labels, Maps, or the website.

One line you can say: *“We take your messy stack and give you a clear, searchable digital book.”*

3. The product (OCR searchable digital book)

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) turns a picture of a page into text a computer can search. The client gets a searchable digital book, not a pile of dumb image scans.

Every package includes

1. Scan or receive pages

2. OCR so words are findable

3. Consistent naming

4. Index / bookmarks where useful

5. Secure link delivery of the master PDF packet

Optional

  • Flash drive (USB) mail-back kit
  • Return of paper originals (freight + handle)
  • Partner shred / dispose only after written approval

Man × Machine

  • Operator scans batches, confirms counts, says when a batch is ready
  • Money Maker runs OCR, naming, index, quality check (QA — Quality Assurance), and packet assembly

Job IDs look like CS-YYYYMMDD-## (ClearStack Job ID). Put the ID on Zelle memos, labels, and folders.

6. Customer journey

1. Quote — Client gives approximate page count (form, email, or call). Same-day package quote with CS-ID.

2. Deposit — 50% to start. Card full deposit or Zelle with $50 courtesy only if deposit > $50 only.

3. Intake — After deposit clears, share packing checklist + CS-ID label + receive path (ship / drop / upload). Never publish home address.

4. Work — Scan → BATCH READY → OCR packet → QA.

5. Deliver — Secure link (included). Optional USB mail-back (+$35).

6. Return / dispose — Client chooses: return paper, pickup, hold window, or partner shred after written OK. Never destroy early.

13. Legal firewall

ClearStack public materials must never mix with personal court or benefits case work.

Spellings for the firewall (plain words once):

  • WCAB — Workers’ Compensation Appeals Board (California workers’ comp court system)
  • SSA — Social Security Administration
  • Related: EAMS (Electronic Adjudication Management System), federal disability lanes, employer case names

Keep off ClearStack

  • Website pages, GBP, outreach emails, invoices, package names, ads, and social posts

Keep in the private operator lane only

  • Personal quizzes, case calendars, and career/case chats

Never email courts, defense counsel, or agencies from ClearStack. ClearStack sells document → searchable PDF packets — nothing else on the public face.

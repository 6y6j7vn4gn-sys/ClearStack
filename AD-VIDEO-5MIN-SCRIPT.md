## Full script (~5:00)

### [0:00–0:20] HOOK — the stack
**Visual:** Slow push on a messy binder / banker box / mixed forms on a cream desk. Soft navy grade. Gold line wipe. Logo mark small corner.

**VO (warm, steady):**  
You’ve got the stack. Leases. Closeout binders. Closed files. Board packets. Receipts in a folder that somehow multiplied.

Filing rooms don’t search. Photo dumps don’t organize. And the big bureau? Slow quotes. Impersonal. Built for warehouses — not your mid-size job.

**On-screen:** *Messy stack in.*

---

### [0:20–0:50] PROMISE — ClearStack book
**Visual:** Same stack → tidy PDF on tablet/laptop. Ctrl+F highlight animation. Bookmarks panel. Cream UI card: “Searchable digital book.” Navy/gold logo full.

**VO:**  
ClearStack turns that mess into a **searchable digital book**.

Not a pile of pictures. A real packet: OCR so you can find a name, a date, an invoice number — fast. Named files. Bookmarks. An index. Clear packet out.

**On-screen:** *Messy stack in. Clear packet out.* · Tagline alternate: *Clear the stack. Keep the signal.*

---

### [0:50–1:40] ALL FORM TYPES
**Visual:** Quick montage (redacted): letter · legal · invoice · application · HR form · batched receipts · statement · contract · mixed binder tabs · phone photos dropping into a PDF. Gold checkmarks.

**VO:**  
We handle the forms you actually have — not just perfect single-sided letter stock.

Letter and legal. Invoices and applications. HR forms. Receipts — batched. Statements. Contracts. Mixed binders. Color or black-and-white to match the originals. Photos into PDF when the jobsite or the shoebox needs it.

Specialty sheets — blueprints, fragile pieces — we quote or partner, then **still fold them into your book**.

**On-screen:** *All common form types · One searchable book*

---

### [1:40–2:40] PACKAGING → SHIP / DROP / UPLOAD
**Visual:** Simple 3-panel graphic (navy cards, gold numbers): ① Pack ② Send ③ We confirm. B-roll: labeling a box with CS- ID · scheduled drop-off handshake (faces optional/anonymous) · upload progress bar.

**VO:**  
Here’s how it works — approachable, fixed scope, no warehouse maze.

**One:** You get a same-day quote from an approximate **page count**. Fifty percent deposit starts the job.

**Two:** You send the paper your way —  
ship a box with tracking,  
schedule a friendly Inland Empire drop-off,  
or upload scans you’ve already shot.

**Three:** We email you the moment it lands. *We have it — here’s your ClearStack job ID.*

No cold walk-in chaos. No mystery truck. Just clear intake.

**On-screen:** *Ship · Drop off · Upload* · *50% to start*

---

### [2:40–3:40] BUILD → LINK → FLASH DRIVE
**Visual:** Hands/tablet capture or feeder B-roll (no PHI). Screen: OCR pass · file names · bookmark tree. Delivery email mock (ClearStack branded). Optional USB + printed index in a simple mailer — gold accent sticker “ClearStack”.

**VO:**  
Then we build the book.

Pages captured with care. Text made searchable. Files named like a human will find them next year. Index and bookmarks so it opens like a document — not a junk drawer.

**Primary delivery is included:** a secure download link or shared folder — your searchable master PDF, plus splits if they’re useful, and an index sheet.

Want peace of mind in your hand? Add a **flash drive mail-back** — drive, ship, printed index. Many first clients take the link **and** the USB. Both is fine.

**On-screen:** *Searchable PDF + index* · *Optional flash drive*

---

### [3:40–4:20] RETURN VS DISPOSE
**Visual:** Split screen. Left: sealed return carton. Right: partner shred / dispose icon with “after you approve.” Soft cream background. Never show destruction of unlabeled “client” paper as spectacle.

**VO:**  
When your digital book is ready, we ask — clearly, twice if needed:

After you approve…  
**A)** Return the originals to you,  
**B)** Securely dispose or shred through a partner — certificate when available,  
**C)** Or hold briefly, then dispose if we don’t hear back.

We never destroy your paper before you sign off on the digital delivery. Your choice. Your custody. Your peace of mind.

**On-screen:** *Return · Dispose · You approve first*

---

### [4:20–4:50] PRICING & FIT
**Visual:** Clean navy price cards with gold borders: $595 · $1,250 · $1,950. Small caption: by page count · rush available. Map silhouette: Upland / IE service area (no street address).

**VO:**  
ClearStack is count-priced packages — transparent, fixed scope.

Up to fifty pages. Fifty to two hundred. Two hundred to five hundred. Rush when capacity allows.

Built for property teams, GCs and trades, title and escrow, office managers who need the packet this week — not a corporate portal and a six-week shrug.

Local Inland Empire care. Zoom walkthrough on delivery if you want to click through your new book together.

**On-screen:** *$595 / $1,250 / $1,950* · *IE service area*

---

### [4:50–5:00] CLOSE — CTA
**Visual:** Full logo on navy. Gold underline. Cream CTA bar. End card holds 5 seconds.

**VO:**  
Messy stack in. Clear packet out.

Tell us your approximate page count. ClearStack will send a same-day quote — and turn your forms into a searchable digital book.

**On-screen end card:**  
**ClearStack**  
Searchable digital books  
*Page count → quote*  
Google · LinkedIn · clearstack (site/contact as published)  
Colors: navy / gold / cream  

**End.**

## Production notes (operator)

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| Length | Target 4:50–5:10; trim montage if needed |
| Music | Soft corporate / acoustic bed — low under VO; no hype EDM |
| Voice | Approachable adult; smile in the tone; not radio-announcer hard sell |
| B-roll safety | Redact names, account numbers, faces of minors; no real client PHI |
| Brand | **ClearStack only** — no personal legal names on cards, VO, or lower thirds |
| Cuts | Prefer gentle dissolves; gold line wipes between major beats |
| Captions | Burned-in or sidecar — many watch muted on LinkedIn |
